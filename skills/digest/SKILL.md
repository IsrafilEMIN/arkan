# Digest

## Read it first

Fetch the full source. If the fetch fails or only part loads (X posts and articles often do), say so and ask the user to paste the text. Never summarize from the title or a preview.

## Output

1. **Core claim** in one or two sentences.
2. **The argument,** topic by topic, in the author's order. Keep the specific numbers, names, mechanisms and examples; cut the repetition and the filler. Mark where the author asserts without evidence.
3. **Practical steps:** concrete actions, each one a verb the user can do this week. If the user named a context (their AI rig, their business plan, their harness), map each step to it.
4. **Where it is weak:** what the author skips, overstates, or what a reader could misapply. One to three points.

Keep the summary shorter than the source, never thinner: "without losing details and sophistication" means compress words, not ideas.

## When the user asks a narrow question

If the user asks about one part ("looking at the intro only", "why would RAG be a waste of time"), answer that part, and quote the line it comes from.
