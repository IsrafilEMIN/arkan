# Mizan lenses

Each lens casts a different light on the same event. For each: the few variables that carry most of the weight, the players, their leverage, and the constraint that breaks them. Use only the lenses the event touches.

## The stack

- **Substrate** (slowest, decides most): faith and civilization, demographics, energy
- **Capability:** technology, military, infrastructure
- **Coordination:** alliances
- **Liquidity:** financial flows
- **Surface** (fastest, most reversible): control, influence, narrative

When surface and substrate diverge, weight the substrate over a 5 to 10 year window. When they align, things move fast.

## Three passes for any event

1. **Who benefits?** Which player, in which lens, gains cash, capability or legitimacy?
2. **Stack check.** Substrate move (slow, durable) or surface move (narrative, reversible)?
3. **Revealed vs stated.** Did the player do what its stated goal predicts? The gap is the signal.

## Lenses

**1. Faith and civilization.** What people will sacrifice for. Variables: elite belief in their own meaning system, fertility rate, sacrifice capacity, elite vs public creed gap. Breaks on: visible elite hypocrisy (Ibn Khaldun on group cohesion decay).

**2. Financial.** Where capital may flow and at what cost. Variables: yield curve and term premium, central bank balance sheets, real rates, dollar index and reserve share, credit creation, debt to GDP, capital flows, gold and bitcoin vs equities, settlement rails (SWIFT, CIPS, mBridge). Players: Fed and Treasury, ECB, BOJ, PBOC, BIS, IMF, sovereign funds, the big asset managers, primary dealers. Breaks on: bond market refusal, currency confidence collapse.

**3. State survival (infinite game).** States play to keep playing. Variables: fertility and dependency ratio, food and energy self-sufficiency, currency sovereignty, defensibility, internal cohesion, elite and public alignment, succession.

**4. Military.** Ability to coerce or deter, actual not advertised. Variables: procurement speed, munitions per month, logistics and basing, nuclear posture, drones, cyber and electronic warfare, recent combat experience. Breaks on: industrial attrition, political will.

**5. Technology.** The leverage layer that shifts every other lens. Variables: leading-edge chip supply, frontier AI capability and deployment, data center power, talent flows, export controls, standards bodies. Breaks on: physics, energy, talent concentration.

**6. Control and influence.** Who shapes perception and who gets cut off from money, speech or movement. Variables: attention share by platform, surveillance reach, de-platforming and de-banking speed, narrative production, digital ID and CBDC posture. Breaks on: trust collapse, exit to other rails.

**7. Energy.** The physical bottom of every economy. Variables: production by source, reserve replacement, refining and LNG capacity, strategic reserves, OPEC+ spare capacity, grid baseload, capex cycle. Breaks on: depletion, underinvestment.

**8. Infrastructure.** Whoever owns the rails owns the toll. Variables: maritime chokepoints (Hormuz, Malacca, Bab el-Mandeb, Suez, Panama), undersea cables, pipelines, rail corridors, payment rails, port concessions.

**9. Alliances.** Force multipliers, only as strong as the weakest shared interest. Variables: treaty depth vs realpolitik, trade integration, intelligence sharing, swap lines, basing, clarity of a common adversary. Breaks on: free riders, diverging threat perception.

Add or drop variables when revealed behavior contradicts the map. Log the change.
